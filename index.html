What is NDVI?

NDVI (Normalized Difference Vegetation Index) is a numerical indicator that uses the visible and near-infrared bands to analyze vegetation health and density.

📡

Sentinel-2 Data

High-resolution satellite imagery from ESA's Sentinel-2 constellation provides accurate and up-to-date vegetation monitoring capabilities.

🎯

Applications

Agricultural monitoring, forest health assessment, environmental research, and climate change studies benefit from NDVI analysis.

About NDVI WebGIS

Advanced vegetation monitoring platform

Our Mission

NDVI WebGIS provides accessible, real-time vegetation monitoring capabilities using advanced satellite imagery and geospatial analysis tools. Our platform democratizes access to high-quality environmental data for researchers, farmers, and environmental professionals.

By leveraging the European Space Agency's Sentinel-2 satellite constellation, we deliver accurate, up-to-date vegetation health information that supports sustainable agriculture, environmental monitoring, and climate research initiatives worldwide.

Technical Specifications

  • 🛰️ Sentinel-2 MSI sensor data (10m resolution)
  • 📊 Real-time NDVI calculation and visualization
  • 🌍 Global coverage with 5-day revisit cycle
  • ☁️ Advanced cloud masking algorithms
  • 📈 Time series analysis and trend detection
  • 💾 Multiple data export formats

NDVI Methodology

📡

Data Acquisition

Sentinel-2 satellites capture multispectral imagery every 5 days, providing consistent global coverage with high spatial and temporal resolution.

🧮

NDVI Calculation

NDVI = (NIR - Red) / (NIR + Red), where NIR is near-infrared reflectance and Red is red light reflectance from vegetation surfaces.

🎨

Visualization

Color-coded maps represent vegetation health, with values ranging from -1 (water/bare soil) to +1 (dense, healthy vegetation).
